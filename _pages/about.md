I am currently an Associate Professor (First Appointment) at South China Agricultural University (SCAU). My research focuses on robust watermarking, reversible data hiding, and generative AI security.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Fellow at Fudan University's Multimedia & Artificial Intelligence Security Laboratory (MAS), supervised by [Prof. Xinpeng Zhang](https://cs.fudan.edu.cn/3f/d2/c25909a278482/page.htm) and [Prof. Zhenxing Qian](https://tomzqian.github.io/). I received my Ph.D. from SCAU in 2024, supervised by [Prof. Chuntao Wang](https://www.scholat.com/wangchuntao), with additional guidance from [Prof. Kangshun Li](https://yjsglxt.scau.edu.cn/open/WxXlbs/TeacherInfo.aspx?jsbh=LiKangShun20).

During my Ph.D., I visited Hong Kong Baptist University and collaborated with [Prof. Yiu-ming Cheung](https://www.comp.hkbu.edu.hk/~ymc). For academic collaboration, please contact me at [yichao_tang@foxmail.com](mailto:yichao_tang@foxmail.com).
